04 / Understand WAFID

What it is.
Why it matters.

WAFID medical screening is generally required before travelling to GCC countries for employment, residence or higher studies. The examination is completed at a WAFID-assigned medical centre, which issues the fitness report.

01

Assigned centres

Your medical examination must be completed at the centre assigned through the WAFID process.

02

Fitness report

The medical centre generates your report after completing the required examination.

03

Guided support

We assist with booking and process guidance; medical decisions remain with the authorised centre.
